The story

A girl from the mountains above the Mediterranean.

Azime grew up in a village in the Taurus Mountains, high above Alanya on the Mediterranean coast. Mornings meant milking, gathering, pressing and baking. The pantry was something you built with your hands over a season.

Today she lives in Cherry Creek North, Denver, loves beautiful things, and believes the old skills are the ultimate luxury. She teaches them to people who never had the chance to learn, with high standards and a little bit of heat.

"My grandmother never measured. You will, until you don't have to."
